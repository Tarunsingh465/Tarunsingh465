> **Data science is more than writing code: it is mathematical reasoning applied to data.**

<details open>
<summary><b>Linear Algebra — representing data and models</b></summary>

- Scalars, vectors, matrices and multidimensional arrays
- Vector operations, dot products and matrix multiplication
- Vector norms, distances and geometric intuition
- Linear transformations
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- Tensor products and complex vector representations
- Implementing mathematical operations with Python and NumPy

*Connections to ML: feature representations, neural network computations, embeddings and dimensionality reduction.*

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>Probability — reasoning about uncertainty</b></summary>

- Sample spaces, events and probability rules
- Conditional probability and Bayes' theorem
- Independent and dependent events
- Random variables and probability distributions
- Expectation, variance and standard deviation
- Joint, marginal and conditional distributions
- Sampling and the intuition behind the Central Limit Theorem

*Connections to data science: uncertainty, probabilistic predictions, classification and statistical modeling.*

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>Statistics — extracting evidence from data</b></summary>

- Descriptive statistics: mean, median, quantiles and dispersion
- Variance, standard deviation, distributions and outliers
- Exploratory data analysis and correlation
- Sampling, estimation and confidence intervals
- Hypothesis testing and p-values
- Statistical significance vs. practical significance
- Regression analysis and residual interpretation

*Connections to data science: data quality, experiment analysis, feature investigation, model evaluation and evidence-based decisions.*

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Calculus & Optimization — how models learn</b></summary>

- Functions, limits and derivatives
- Partial derivatives and gradients
- Chain rule and the intuition behind backpropagation
- Loss functions and gradient-based optimization
- Learning rates, convergence and regularization
- Numerical optimization with SciPy

*Connections to AI: neural network training, loss minimization and parameter optimization.*

</details>
